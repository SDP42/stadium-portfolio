# Accessibility

The 3D stadium is the centrepiece, but every piece of content is real HTML and reachable without it.

- **Keyboard:** every zone, link and button is focusable, with a visible focus ring. Arrow keys and Page Up/Down walk the tour, Esc zooms out or closes the menu and badge cabinet, and Space shoots in the shootout.
- **Skip link:** the first Tab stop jumps straight to the projects.
- **Semantics:** the active nav item carries `aria-current="page"`, panels are labelled sections, the scene card is a polite live region and the penalty result is assertive.
- **Titles:** each view sets its own document title, such as `MIRAGE · Swayam Panchal`.
- **Reduced motion:** with `prefers-reduced-motion`, camera orbits stop, flights are short, the stinger wipe and ticker are off and pulses are still.
- **Contrast:** panels sit on near-opaque dark glass so text stays readable over the bright 3D scene.
- **No JavaScript:** a fallback lists GitHub, LinkedIn and the résumé.
- **Sound:** off by default; captions in the scene cards carry the same information.
